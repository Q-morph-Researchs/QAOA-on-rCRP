ABOUT THIS FOLDER: POSTHOC
==========================

Results of the noiseless angle and depth study (paper Section 3.6). Nothing
here ran on hardware.
- Table_A6.md: the paper's Appendix Table A6.
- results.json: everything behind it, including every tuned angle vector,
  where its search started, how many starts and evaluations were used, and
  the sensitivity percentiles.
