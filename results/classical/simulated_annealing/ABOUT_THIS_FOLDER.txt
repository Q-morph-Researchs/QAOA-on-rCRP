ABOUT THIS FOLDER: SIMULATED_ANNEALING
======================================

Simulated annealing (paper Section 2.7.3, Table 5 and Figure 3).
- PROTOCOL_PREREGISTERED.json: the settings, fixed before any run (2,000
  candidates per run, temperature falling from 1 to 0.01, seeds 0 to 4).
- C_summary.json: the summary for each pairing.
- runs/: one file per run (8 pairings x 5 seeds = 40): seed, starting bit
  string, the plan it selected (its best legal candidate) and whether that
  plan was optimal.
- traces/: one CSV per run with every step, accepted or rejected. Columns:
  step number, proposed bit string and its energy, temperature, random
  number, energy change, accepted or not, current bit string, and the
  decoded verdict.

HEADS-UP
The random-number states were reconstructed from the seeds after the runs;
they weren't saved during the runs. The traces themselves are complete.

NAMES USED IN THE FILES
- random-S3T4C6-21 = bay A, random-S3T4C6-24 = bay B, random-S3T4C7-32 = bay
  C, random-S4T3C6-16 = bay D, data3-3-8 = bay E.
- "galle" = the QUBO-based encoding (named after the Galle et al. model).
  "predicate" = the predicate-based encoding.
- "accepted" = what the paper calls a legal plan. "replay-optimal" = an
  optimal plan.
