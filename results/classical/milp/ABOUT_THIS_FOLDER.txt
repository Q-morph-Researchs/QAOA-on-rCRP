ABOUT THIS FOLDER: MILP
=======================

The exact MILP results (paper Section 2.7.1). Each bay was solved twice:
"plain" (the original model) and "with_preprocessing" (after our presolve).
Both give the same optimum. Each JSON file has the optimum, the bound, the
gap, the number of nodes, the run time and the replay check of the rebuilt
crane plan. A_summary.json and A_summary.csv summarize all five bays.

Optima: 1, 2, 1, 2 and 2 relocations for bays A to E.

NAMES USED IN THE FILES
- random-S3T4C6-21 = bay A, random-S3T4C6-24 = bay B, random-S3T4C7-32 = bay
  C, random-S4T3C6-16 = bay D, data3-3-8 = bay E.
- "galle" = the QUBO-based encoding (named after the Galle et al. model).
  "predicate" = the predicate-based encoding.
- "accepted" = what the paper calls a legal plan. "replay-optimal" = an
  optimal plan.
