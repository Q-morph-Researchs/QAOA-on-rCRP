ABOUT THIS FOLDER: CALIBRATION_CHECK
====================================

Checks, for all 40 hardware jobs, that each one used its frozen angle
record, the right circuit files and the right compiled circuit, and that the
rates recomputed from the raw results match the saved summary (paper
Sections 2.4 and 2.5). Read CALIBRATION_BRIDGE.md; calibration_bridge.json
has the same results in machine-readable form.
