ABOUT THIS FOLDER: CLASSICAL
============================

Short version: "does a normal computer do better?" (As the paper says: yes.)
Paper Section 2.7 and Table 5.

Start with RESULTS.md. It's a one-page verdict table that points to the
evidence for each claim.

WHAT'S IN HERE
- milp/: the exact MILP results.
- greedy/: the greedy rule results.
- simulated_annealing/: simulated annealing, including every step of every
  run.
- calibration_check/: proof that all 40 hardware jobs used their frozen
  angles and circuits.

HEADS-UP
SA versus QAOA is a "same number of guesses" comparison, not a "same cost"
one: each SA run evaluates 2,000 candidates and each QAOA job has 2,000
shots. That's the only thing matched, and the paper says so.

NAMES USED IN THE FILES
- random-S3T4C6-21 = bay A, random-S3T4C6-24 = bay B, random-S3T4C7-32 = bay
  C, random-S4T3C6-16 = bay D, data3-3-8 = bay E.
- "galle" = the QUBO-based encoding (named after the Galle et al. model).
  "predicate" = the predicate-based encoding.
- "accepted" = what the paper calls a legal plan. "replay-optimal" = an
  optimal plan.
