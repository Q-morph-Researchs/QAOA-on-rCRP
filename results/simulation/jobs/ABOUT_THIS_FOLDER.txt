ABOUT THIS FOLDER: JOBS
=======================

One folder per hardware job (40), each with:
- ideal_states.npy: 2,000 shots sampled from the noiseless circuit.
- noisy_states.npy: 2,000 shots from the noise-model simulation of that
  job's compiled circuit.
- result.json: the seeds, the random-number-generator state before and
  after, the legal and optimal scores, and the simulator settings. Seeds
  come from SeedSequence 2026101701 (paper Section 2.8 and Appendix B.7).
