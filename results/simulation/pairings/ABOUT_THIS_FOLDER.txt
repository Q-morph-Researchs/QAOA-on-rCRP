ABOUT THIS FOLDER: PAIRINGS
===========================

One folder per pairing (8), with exact results over every possible bit
string:
- exact.json: the angles, the numbers of legal and optimal bit strings, the
  exact uniform rates and the exact ideal probabilities. These are the
  "uniform" and "ideal" values in the paper's Tables 4 and 6.
- legal_plans.json: every legal bit string (as an integer) with the crane
  plan it decodes to.
- state_space.npz: for every possible bit string, whether it's legal or
  optimal, its relocation count, its energy and its ideal probability.

NAMES USED IN THE FILES
- random-S3T4C6-21 = bay A, random-S3T4C6-24 = bay B, random-S3T4C7-32 = bay
  C, random-S4T3C6-16 = bay D, data3-3-8 = bay E.
- "galle" = the QUBO-based encoding (named after the Galle et al. model).
  "predicate" = the predicate-based encoding.
- "accepted" = what the paper calls a legal plan. "replay-optimal" = an
  optimal plan.
