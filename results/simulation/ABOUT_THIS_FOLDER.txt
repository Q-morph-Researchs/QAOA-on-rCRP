ABOUT THIS FOLDER: SIMULATION
=============================

The ideal and noise-model simulations of the same circuits that ran on
hardware (paper Section 2.8, Table 6 and Figure 4).
- jobs/: simulated shots for each of the 40 jobs.
- pairings/: exact results over every possible bit string, for each pairing.
- noise_models/: the noise model and circuit audit for each circuit version.
- SIMULATION_PROTOCOL.json: the frozen settings (seeds, noise-model
  settings, limits).
- environment.json: the software versions used.

HEADS-UP
The noise model has depolarizing gate errors and readout errors from IBM's
calibration data, but no explicit thermal relaxation (T1/T2), because IBM's
snapshot had no qubit frequencies. It also leaves out crosstalk, leakage and
drift. That's why the hardware lost more than the model predicted; the paper
discusses this.
