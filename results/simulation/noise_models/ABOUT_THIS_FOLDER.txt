ABOUT THIS FOLDER: NOISE_MODELS
===============================

One folder per circuit version that ran (10), each with:
- noise_model.json: the noise model built from that version's IBM
  calibration snapshot.
- audit.json: the physical qubits used, the compiled two-qubit gate count
  and depth, the circuit and snapshot hashes, and the error records used for
  the noise model and the ESP. This is where Appendix Table A4 comes from.

NAMES USED IN THE FILES
- random-S3T4C6-21 = bay A, random-S3T4C6-24 = bay B, random-S3T4C7-32 = bay
  C, random-S4T3C6-16 = bay D, data3-3-8 = bay E.
- "galle" = the QUBO-based encoding (named after the Galle et al. model).
  "predicate" = the predicate-based encoding.
- "accepted" = what the paper calls a legal plan. "replay-optimal" = an
  optimal plan.
