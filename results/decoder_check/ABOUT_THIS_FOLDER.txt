ABOUT THIS FOLDER: DECODER_CHECK
================================

The evidence that an independently written decoder (code/decoder_check/)
agrees with the main one (paper Section 2.3.3 and Appendix B.4).
- EXHAUSTIVE_RESULTS.json: all 46,336 possible bit strings of the eight
  pairings. The legal or illegal verdicts, relocation counts and move lists
  all match.
- HARDWARE_DECODE_CHECK.json: all 40 hardware jobs (80,000 shots)
  re-decoded. The legal and optimal counts all match.
- ENCODING_CONTRACT.json: the one thing both decoders share, namely which
  qubit stands for which model variable after presolve. Because of it, this
  checks the step from bits to crane plan, not the presolve itself.
