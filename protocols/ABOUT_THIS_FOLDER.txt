ABOUT THIS FOLDER: PROTOCOLS
============================

Short version: the rules we wrote down before touching the quantum computer.
All three files are from 10 October 2026, two days before the first IBM job.

WHAT'S IN HERE
- STUDY_PROTOCOL.md: version 1 of the plan. The question, the frozen inputs,
  the checks every circuit had to pass before submission, and the compiler
  settings (seed 11 is the one we ran).
- STUDY_PROTOCOL_v2.md: version 2, same day. Picks the five bays as a "size
  ladder" from 158 to 418 compiled two-qubit gates (paper Appendix B.2).
- IBM_RUN_PLAN_2026_10_10.pdf: the run plan. Which bays and encodings, 40
  jobs, the job order and the QPU-time cap.

THE ONE THING THAT LOOKS LIKE A CONTRADICTION
Protocol v2 says QUBO is only a resource comparison, with no QUBO hardware
run. The run plan, written later that day after the noiseless and
fake-device simulations but before any hardware job, added QUBO for the
three bays whose circuits fit under 400 compiled two-qubit gates. The paper
describes exactly this in Appendix B.2.

HEADS-UP
The plan said all jobs would run on one day. They actually went in on 12, 14
and 16 October, with the circuits recompiled each time. The paper says so
(Section 2.5 and Limitations).

NAMES USED IN THE FILES
- random-S3T4C6-21 = bay A, random-S3T4C6-24 = bay B, random-S3T4C7-32 = bay
  C, random-S4T3C6-16 = bay D, data3-3-8 = bay E.
- "galle" = the QUBO-based encoding (named after the Galle et al. model).
  "predicate" = the predicate-based encoding.
- "accepted" = what the paper calls a legal plan. "replay-optimal" = an
  optimal plan.
