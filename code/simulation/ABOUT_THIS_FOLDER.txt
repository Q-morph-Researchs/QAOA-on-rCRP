ABOUT THIS FOLDER: SIMULATION
=============================

- run_simulations.py: simulates every hardware job twice, once ideal (no
  noise) and once with a noise model built from IBM's calibration snapshot
  for that job, with frozen seeds (paper Section 2.8). It never contacts
  IBM.
- resource_scaling.py: counts the qubits and two-qubit gates our encodings
  would need for benchmark bays with 12 to 20 containers (Appendix Table
  A7). It only counts; it doesn't simulate or solve anything. The results
  are in results/simulation/ and results/tables/resource_scaling.json.
