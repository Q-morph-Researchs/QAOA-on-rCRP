ABOUT THIS FOLDER: CLASSICAL
============================

The classical comparison methods (paper Section 2.7). None of them uses any
QAOA code.
- galle_milp.py: the exact MILP (the Galle et al. CRP-I model, solved with
  HiGHS through SciPy, no hints and zero optimality gap).
- greedy.py: the greedy rule.
- sa_matched.py: simulated annealing with 2,000 candidates per run, the same
  number as the shots in one QAOA job.
- calibration_bridge.py: checks that all 40 hardware jobs used their frozen
  angles and circuits.
- common.py, sim9_bridge.py: shared helpers and a read-only link to
  Sim9_2.py for building the energy tables. The results are in
  results/classical/.
